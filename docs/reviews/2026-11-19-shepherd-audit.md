Result: **live DB ≡ `backend/app/models.py` ≡ `docs/erd.md` ≡ `docs/data-dictionary.md`** — no mismatch found. `alembic check`: "No new upgrade operations detected". 11 schema tests pass; round-trip and the one-step-downgrade NOT NULL inverse verified on a scratch DB today. 5 tables: `properties`(6 cols), `bookings`(30), `reviews`(15), `guest_requests`(7), `kpi_daily`(8).
